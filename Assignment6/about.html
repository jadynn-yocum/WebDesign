The Story Behind the Lens

Originally from Wisconsin, my obsession with capturing moments started early. I grew up watching my mom take photos everywhere we went, and I always loved capturing raw moments. Creating things is just what I do, and I truly believe it's where I shine brightest. I dove way deeper into visual arts during my late teens, and as I've gotten older, I've fallen in love with exploring the world of videography.

I eventually moved out to Montana to fully immerse myself in everything its amazing public lands have to offer. After graduating with my Bachelor’s in Media Arts, my next adventure is heading back toward the Great Lakes to explore and document the Upper Peninsula of Michigan and the rugged shores of Lake Superior. Traveling and storytelling are entirely in my blood, and I hope to keep capturing the world in a deeply meaningful way.

Blending a raw love for the outdoors with clean, modern media design and visual storytelling.

My Creative Philosophy

Natural Balance

I love building brand styles with deep earth tones and rich textures inspired by the wild spaces I explore. Dropping sterile defaults makes room for warmth and breathing space in a clean layout.

Real Storytelling

Visual projects have to do more than just look pretty—they need to connect. Whether I'm editing a video sequence, setting up a print layout, or designing a high-contrast web asset, raw intent shapes all my choices.

Meaningful Curation

My photography, video frames, and mixed-media layouts are built to honor untouched environments. Every piece focuses on tracking natural lighting shifts and sharing local wilderness stories.
